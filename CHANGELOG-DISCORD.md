# The Howling Whispers - What You Need To Know

## Version 0.2.0 - Smarter Story Context

- Coda, Heather, Peony, and Senako now have structured, server-authoritative world lorebooks.
- Custom and imported characters receive a conservative scene-based world-context fallback.
- The app automatically activates only the world details relevant to the current scene and conversation for every character.
- Lore selection is deterministic and requires no extra AI request.
- **Peek Context** appears in the story sidebar after generation and shows active canon, active lore, retained history, revisions, and estimated token use.
- Mandatory character identity and safety remain protected before lore or old history receives context space.
- Sandbox still starts without preset world lore.
- Version checks now use full semantic-version precedence, including correct prerelease handling.

## Coda's World Is Open For Review

- Coda's character screen now contains her early-steam, old-rune world guide.
- Eight illustrated opening scenes range from quiet neighborhood mysteries to dangerous new machinery.
- Each scene has its own landscape artwork featuring Coda and its location.
- Choose Trusted Companion, Runesmith's Apprentice, Pumping-House Mechanic, Traveler in the Rain, or write a Custom Role before starting.
- Your selected role is saved with the new story and supplies external context without deciding your personality, feelings, dialogue, or choices.
- The origin of Coda's collar, its red pendant, and her ancient lineage remain unresolved mysteries.

## Peony Has Her Full Character Depth

- Peony now uses a carefully structured version of her original character canon.
- Her voice, trust, boundaries, interests, secrets, and relationship progression should stay much more consistent.
- Existing Peony stories use the latest character revision automatically. You do not need to restart them.
- Private adult material is stored separately and stays out of ordinary scenes.

## Better Long-Story Continuity

- Relationship labels and bond progress now influence generation instead of being display-only.
- When a conversation becomes very long, older dialogue is trimmed before core character identity and safety rules.
- The newest turns are kept so replies continue from the current moment.

## Recent Features

- Open Sandbox starts without a preset scene, memories, or opening move.
- Local GPU generation is available through Ollama and Mistral Nemo 12B.
- Stories can be resumed or deleted, and custom scenes can be deleted with their linked sessions.
- Chat font size and the character/context side panels can be adjusted independently.
- The entrance rotates featured characters, with an option to keep Coda.

## Need To Know

- Characters, sessions, messages, settings, and saved NovelAI tokens remain local to this browser profile.
- Clearing browser site data also clears those stories and settings.
- Direct remote test mode uses unencrypted HTTP. Disable it after testing and do not use it for sensitive stories or tokens.
