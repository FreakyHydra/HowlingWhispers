# Senako Steel - integration record

Senako Steel is already integrated into The Howling Whispers. This folder keeps
the cleaned structured source used by the built-in character and world lore.

## Included files

- `senako-steel.addon.json` - cleaned character data, opening scene, memories,
  roleplay rules, examples, public asset paths, and minor-safety boundaries.
- `Senako Steel.original.json` - the original supplied character card, kept
  locally and excluded from Git.

Runtime images live once under `public/assets/` rather than being duplicated here.

## Safety

Senako is twelve. Her roleplay must stay strictly friendship/family-oriented and
age-appropriate, following the boundaries in the structured source.
